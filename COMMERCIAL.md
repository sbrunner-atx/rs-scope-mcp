# Commercial use

rs-scope-mcp is free for hobbyists. Commercial use needs a license.

rs-scope-mcp is licensed under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). The
[LICENSE](LICENSE) file is the binding text. This page explains it in plain words and lists what
you can buy. If the two ever seem to disagree, or you are not sure which side of the line you are
on, ask before you rely on either.

## Free to use

You do not need a license, a key or a registration to use rs-scope-mcp for:

- **Personal use and hobby projects.** Your own oscilloscope, your own bench, your own learning.
- **Amateur radio.** Your station, a club station, Field Day and contests, and volunteer
  emergency communication.
- **Education.** Schools, colleges and universities, their students and teachers, and courses
  that teach electronics, radio or embedded systems.
- **Nonprofit and public organizations.** Charitable organizations, public research
  organizations, public safety and health organizations, environmental protection organizations
  and government institutions.

Free use includes changing the software and sharing it, as long as whoever gets a copy also gets
the license and the `Required Notice:` line.

## Needs a commercial license

Any use by a business or for a business needs a commercial license. That includes:

- **Internal use.** Engineers using it on the company's oscilloscopes, in development, on a test
  bench, in production test, or in a lab.
- **Work for clients.** Consultants, contractors, design houses and test labs using it on
  customer hardware or in customer projects.
- **Products and services.** Shipping it with test equipment or a product, building it into an
  automated test system, or running it as part of a service you sell.
- **Business use on a personal machine.** If the work is for your employer or your customers, it
  needs a license, even on your own laptop and your own time.

If you are not sure, assume a license is needed and ask. Most questions take one email.

## What you can buy

- **Commercial license.** Annual, per company. Covers the company and its employees, with every
  release published during the term.
- **Support for more instruments.** Other Rohde & Schwarz oscilloscope families, or other
  vendors' instruments, brought to the same standard: complete command registry, firmware
  overlay, bench-verified behavior and a simulator, verified on your hardware.
- **Custom skills and integrations.** Measurement and test procedures written as skills for your
  products, data export into your tools, and integration with your test automation.
- **Support plans.** Help with deployment, answers when an instrument does something unexpected,
  and priority fixes.
